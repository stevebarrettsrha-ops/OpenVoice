OPENVOICE

Say it in any voice.

OpenVoice clones the tone colour of a reference clip and speaks your text with it. Accent and emotion come from the base voice you pick, not from the clip.

A few things to set up first

PyTorch and the OpenVoice checkpoints are not here yet. The Engine page installs them into this app's own environment, one button each.

Speakers

Each speaker gets a voice (a clip from the Voices page, or none to keep the base voice) and a base: the language and accent on V2, the language and emotion on V1. Lines are read by the base voice first, then converted to the clip's tone colour.

Voices

Add a voice from a clip

A clean clip of one person, 5 to 30 seconds, no music and no long silences, makes the best voice. The clip's language does not matter. The tone-colour embedding is worked out once, the first time the voice is used, and cached. Upstream has a QnA on clips that do not clone well.
